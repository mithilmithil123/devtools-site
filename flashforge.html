ℹ️ How to Use

  1. Download or select your ISO file (Ubuntu, Fedora, Windows, etc.)
  2. Click "Choose File" or drag-and-drop the ISO onto the upload area
  3. Click "Detect USB Devices" to find connected USB drives
  4. Select the USB drive you want to flash
  5. Review the warning and click "Start Flashing"
  6. Wait for the flashing process to complete (can take 5-15 minutes)
  7. Safely eject the USB drive and use it to boot your system
